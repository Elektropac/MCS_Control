# MCS Control — User Guide

This guide is for drivers and operators who fuel vehicles at sites equipped with MCS Control. No technical knowledge required.

---

## Fuelling with the MCS Fuel App (Phone)

The MCS Fuel app connects to the pump controller via Bluetooth.

### Steps

1. **Open** the MCS Fuel app on your phone
2. **Stand near** the pump (within 5 metres)
3. **Tap the pump** shown in the app to connect
4. **Authorise** — enter your PIN or use biometrics
5. **Lift the nozzle** and fuel your vehicle
6. **Replace the nozzle** when done
7. The app shows your total litres and confirms the transaction

### Tips

- Keep Bluetooth enabled on your phone
- The app will show "Searching..." if you're too far from the pump
- You can see your fuelling history in the app

---

## Fuelling with the Console (Card/Tag)

If your site has an MCS Console terminal:

### Steps

1. **Scan** your access card or key tag on the reader
2. **Select the pump** (if more than one is available)
3. **Lift the nozzle** and fuel your vehicle
4. **Replace the nozzle** when done
5. The Console screen confirms your total

### Tips

- Hold your card flat against the reader for 1–2 seconds
- If the screen shows "Not authorised", contact your fleet manager
- Some sites require you to enter an odometer reading before fuelling

## Reading the Display

The small screen on the MCS Control unit shows:

| Screen | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| Dashboard | Pump status (idle/running), tank levels |
| Pump active | Current flow rate, volume dispensed |
| Tank levels | Bar graph showing fill percentage and litres |
| Error | Problem description with error code |

---

## Buzzer Sounds

| Sound | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Single short beep | Action confirmed (pump started, card accepted) |
| Two short beeps | Transaction complete |
| Three rapid beeps | Warning — check display for details |
| Long continuous tone | Error — pump stopped, see display |

## Common Issues

### Pump Doesn't Start

1. Make sure the nozzle is fully lifted from its cradle
2. Check that you are authorised (app shows "Ready" or Console shows "Pump ready")
3. If the display shows an error, note the message and contact your site manager
4. Try replacing and re-lifting the nozzle

### App Can't Connect

1. Check that Bluetooth is enabled on your phone
2. Move closer to the pump (within 3–5 metres)
3. Close and reopen the app
4. If the problem persists, try fuelling via the Console terminal instead
5. Contact your site manager if neither method works

### Card Not Recognised

1. Hold the card steady against the reader for 2 seconds
2. Try a different orientation
3. Check with your fleet manager that your card is active
4. If the reader makes no sound at all, it may be offline — contact site support

## Safety Reminders

- **No smoking** while fuelling
- **Turn off your engine** before fuelling
- **Do not overfill** — the system will stop at your authorised limit or when the nozzle auto-shuts
- **Report spills** immediately to your site manager

---

## Need Help?

Contact your site manager or fleet administrator. They can check your access rights, view transaction history, and resolve most issues remotely.
